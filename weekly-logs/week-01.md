## One concept I can now explain
**Phishing vs Spear Phishing:** Phishing is mass-broadcast 
bait — same email to thousands hoping someone clicks. 
Spear phishing is targeted — the attacker researches 
you specifically (your name, your company, your manager's name) 
to make the message believable. The difference is precision, 
not technique.
