## Most significant concept I learned
Legacy applications are apps that are still running on outdated services or dependencies because using the later versions or updated ones are either costly, time consuming or complex to implement.


## Most useful confusion I resolved
From the term "key exchange" I see it as just the sharing of keys to ensure trusted communication. But that's just an explanation of cryptography - Key exchange involves the sharing of a secret over an insecure channel without transmitting the secret. Asymmetric key handles the transmission while symmetric key handles the actual data. HTTPS uses exactly this.
