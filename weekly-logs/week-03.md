## Most significant concept I learned
Secure enclave is an isolated protected region inside a processor that runs separately from the main OS for storing sensitive data, so that even if the OS is breached the data on the enclave remains safe and untouched. Apple uses it to store fingerprint and FaceID data. 


## Most useful confusion I resolved
Web of trust and root of trust seemed to be the same concept. Understand the difference now: root of trust is a centralized authority validating you as trust-worthy before certifying your identity/key as legitimate - It's what CA's use. Web of trust is decentralized and peer-to-peer , it's a way of establishing trust to a party you're unknown too - a third-party that trusts you vouches for you instead.
